Why This Story Had to Be Told

This book was not born out of imagination alone; it was carved from memory, displacement, and resilience. Walking through borders and oceans leaves marks that time cannot erase.

I wrote this story to give a voice to millions who carry home in an unspoken suitcase, searching for peace without losing who they are.
